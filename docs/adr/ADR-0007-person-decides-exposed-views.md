# ADR-0007: The person decides what is exposed

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-17
- Drafted by: owner

## Context

UC10 (yearly HR assessment) and Michaela's read-only access to everyone's profile conflict with APR-0001.

## Decision

Anne decides to expose a view of her curriculum / skills tracking: it's her data. She can decide to filter out some dimensions: skills related to an occupation, skill domains, dates, etc.
